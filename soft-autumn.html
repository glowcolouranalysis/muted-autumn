Muted Autumn is warm-neutral and low intensity. Your colours sit in that beautifully balanced middle ground, carrying warmth without being distinctly warm, and softness without being cool. They have a muted, slightly dusty quality that makes everything feel naturally harmonious. If it looks bright, icy, sharp, or high-contrast, it is not yours. If it looks warm, muted, blended, and natural, you are right at home.

This is inspiration, not rules. Every colour in your palette works with you and with each other. Use this guide as a reference and a starting point as you learn your colours. Colour analysis is about empowerment, not restriction. Take what feels right, when it feels right, and leave what doesn't.

Your palette is limitless. The Muted Autumn palette contains thousands of colours. The swatches here are carefully chosen examples to help you understand the qualities to look for — warm or neutral-warm, muted, and naturally harmonious. When you shop, use these as a visual compass. Muted Autumn flows into Summer, which means you can borrow some muted Summer tones. Keep them warm-leaning or neutral. If a colour looks cool, icy, or bright, it has gone too far.

Your essence: Effortless. Earthy. Elegant. Relaxed. Natural. Understated. Harmonious. You were perhaps reaching for colours that felt sharp or saturated and wondering why something was slightly off. Your palette gives you permission to settle into softness and to wear it like the quiet confidence it is.

Foundation & Tinted Moisturiser
Your most important foundation rule: warm or neutral-warm undertone only. Golden, peachy, or creamy-based foundations work best. Pink-based or cool foundations will look ashy and fight your natural warmth.
What to look for
Ask for golden, warm, peachy, or neutral-warm undertone in your shade. Avoid shades described as pink, cool, or neutral-cool. Tip: when testing foundation, a warm match that looks alive and natural is right. One that looks ashy, grey, or pink-toned is too cool for you.
Eyes
Your eyes thrive with warm, muted depth and soft definition. Taupes, warm browns, soft olives, and muted bronzes are your most flattering shadow tones. Nothing sharp or high-contrast, blended and earthy is your signature.
Eyeshadow — what works
Base shades: warm ivory, soft peach, oat beige, light caramel. Depth: warm taupe, soft chocolate, muted olive, earthy brown. Accents: soft bronze, muted copper, dusty gold, autumn sage. Matte and satin finishes are your most natural-looking options. Avoid: cool grey, silver, icy lavender, stark black, or any cool metallic.
Eyeliner
Warm medium brown or soft taupe is your most flattering liner. For more definition, try soft chocolate or warm espresso. Avoid cool grey, blue-black, or stark black liner, these create a harshness that works against your natural softness. Tip: a soft warm brown liner keeps the look blended and effortless, which is perfectly Muted Autumn.
Mascara
Warm brown or soft brown-black mascara is your most flattering choice. Pure cool black can work but may feel slightly harsh, if you use it, pair with softer warm eye makeup to balance. Try: any brown or brown-black shade for a rich, natural finish.
Brows & Beard
Brow guidance
Match your brow product to your natural hair colour, or go one shade warmer. A warm medium brown or soft taupe brow product works beautifully. Avoid anything with ash, cool, or grey undertones, these fight your warmth. Tip: ask for "warm brown" or "soft warm taupe" when shopping brow pencils or powders.
Beard & grooming colour
Muted Autumn colouring suits well-kept facial hair beautifully. If you colour your beard or brows, look for soft warm brown, medium brown, or warm chestnut shades. Avoid anything with ash, cool, or blue-black tones. Tip: matte finishes and soft, natural lines suit Muted Autumn best, avoid harsh edges or overly groomed looks.
Hair Colour
Your most beautiful hair colour is often your most natural one. Muted Autumn's natural colouring tends toward warm medium browns, soft auburns, and light chestnuts, and that is exactly what flatters you most.
Your best hair colours
Soft and warm: warm medium brown, light chestnut, soft auburn, and golden brown are all beautiful. These echo your natural warmth and depth. Avoid: blue-black, very dark espresso, icy highlights, or anything that introduces harshness or coolness to your hair.
Highlights & colour techniques
If you highlight, choose soft warm caramel, honey, or light auburn tones only. Keep them subtle and blended, Muted Autumn suits low-contrast, naturally diffused colour. Avoid: cool ash highlights, platinum balayage, or any technique that introduces icy or high-contrast tones. Softness and warmth are your strengths.
What to ask your colourist
Ask for "warm toned," "no ash," "soft," and "blended" for your colour. Show your colourist your palette, having your actual colour reference makes the conversation much easier. Tip: when in doubt, go warmer and softer rather than cooler or higher-contrast.

Is it suitable for Muted Autumn?" Then do a visual check by comparing the product swatch against your palette. Trust your eye.
